Information collectedWhen you sign up: name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way encrypted hash), and the date and version of each consent you give.

International transfers

Your information may be stored and processed outside your country, including in the Republic of Korea and the country where our hosting provider operates. We apply safeguards required by applicable law to such transfers.

Videos from third parties

When you play an embedded video, the platform that hosts it (for example YouTube) may collect information under its own privacy policy. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed mode where available.

YouTube API Services

KREELZ uses YouTube API Services. We use them only to read public information about videos — titles, channel names, thumbnails, and view, like and comment counts — to choose and order the videos we show. We do not access, collect or store any information about your YouTube or Google account, and there is no Google sign-in on KREELZ. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube's embedded player may store or read cookies on your device; you can delete or block them in your browser settings. If we ever ask to connect your Google account, you will be able to revoke that access at any time at Google security settings.

Your rights

Children

The Service is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security and contact

We protect your information with encryption in transit, hashed passwords and access controls. Data protection contact: kreelzkorea@gmail.com.
